01. Data Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you use our services. This includes personal identifiers such as your name, email address, and company details provided during the consultation process.

Automatically collected data may include your IP address, browser type, and interaction metrics with our digital platforms to ensure operational precision.

02. Usage of Information

The information we collect is utilized to deliver, maintain, and enhance our software services. We employ this data to:

  • Provide engineering support and maintain operational security.
  • Analyze usage patterns to refine our insights and forge better solutions.
  • Communicate crucial service updates and administrative information.

03. Cookies & Tracking

We utilize cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our service and hold certain information. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent.

However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our sophisticated digital architecture.

04. Data Security

The security of your data is paramount to our operational ethos. We employ commercially reasonable security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or unauthorized destruction of the data.

While we strive to use premium, industry-standard means to protect your Personal Data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.
