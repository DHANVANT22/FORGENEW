Optimizing Query Performance at Scale

By Engineering Team • Oct 24, 2024 • Database

As our data footprint grew, we started noticing significant degradation in our core analytical queries. What used to take milliseconds was suddenly taking seconds, blocking critical rendering paths on our dashboards. The root cause was a series of complex joins on tables that had recently crossed the billion-row threshold without appropriate indexing strategies.

We needed a systematic approach to identify the bottlenecks. We utilized PostgreSQL's `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` to visualize the execution plan. The immediate finding was a glaring sequential scan dominating the execution time, paired with an inefficient nested loop join.

By identifying the sequential scan on `events_log`, we were able to implement a targeted composite index based on the `tenant_id` and `timestamp` columns. The subsequent 'After index' plan showed a dramatic shift to an Index Only Scan, reducing the overall query cost by two orders of magnitude and resolving the dashboard rendering delays.
